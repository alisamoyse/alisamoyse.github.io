Starchaser

Interactive Web Experience · p5.js · 2026

An interactive browser experience exploring authority, persistence, and choice. Players are repeatedly encouraged to complete an impossible task, but the experience only ends when they decide to stop trying.

02

Tree of Light

Interactive Projection · TouchDesigner · 2026

A collaborative interactive installation, in which a projected tree responds to the people and sound around it. It started as a group experiment with the idea of making digital art feel alive and responsive, and telling a message about how each step we take creates beautiful changes in life.

Using TouchDesigner, we created a tree that grows and changes based on movement and sound around it. As people approach the projection, new branches appear, while audio creates bursts of light and color. I especially liked this project because it was one of my first experiences creating something interactive that changed depending on the people experiencing it.

TouchDesigner Projection Motion Tracking Interactive Art
03

Seasoned

Immersive VR Environment · Unity · 2026

Seasoned is a collaborative VR experience inspired by the canal towns of Jiangnan, China. The larger experience follows one village through four seasons, with each environment using its own lighting, vegetation, weather, soundscape, and interaction.

I created the project's expansive winter environment in Unity, building a snow-covered water town surrounded by mountains and incorporating frozen waterways, falling snow, atmospheric fog, architecture, terrain, and environmental details for an immersive VR experience.

Techno Lights

Music Production · Ableton Live · 2026

My first fully produced track, created while learning Ableton Live. Although I had previous experience playing instruments, this project was my introduction to building a complete song digitally through arrangement, synthesis, layering, and mixing.

I built the track around a house-inspired rhythmic foundation, experimenting with electronic sounds and learning how separate musical elements can be arranged into a cohesive production.

05

Shaina's Tribute

Video Editing · InShot · 2024

A personal short film created from years of family archives for my cousin's 17th birthday. I curated more than 150 photographs and video clips into a cohesive narrative.

I edited the entire project in InShot, organizing the clips in a highly detailed structure while matching the pacing, creating the transitions, and layering meaningful audio moments to the music. This project was less about learning a new piece of software and more about using editing to create something personal, emotional, and meaningful for someone I care about.

ABOUT ME

Beyond the projects.

I like working where people, technology, and ideas come together.

WHO I AM

I'm a Psychological & Brain Sciences and Economics & Accounting student at UC Santa Barbara, with a minor in Media Arts and Design. My interests don't fit neatly into one field — and that's exactly what I like about them.

I'm drawn to understanding how people think and behave while also exploring how design, technology, and media can shape the way we experience the world.

WHAT I DO

As a neuroscience research assistant, I work with VR, EEG, behavioral data, and Unity to study attention and interaction with immersive technology.

Outside the lab, I create interactive websites, digital experiences, videos, and other media. I enjoy taking an idea from concept to execution and figuring out the technology needed to make it real.
